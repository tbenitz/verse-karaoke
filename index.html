How a song gets words

1. Filename or search hits LRCLIB for real synced lyrics.
2. If that misses, browser Whisper drafts a transcript.
3. DeepSeek cleans the wording and splits Verse, Chorus, Bridge.
Word lights follow the timestamps. You can still edit the LRC.
